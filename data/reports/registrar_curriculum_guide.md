# Registrar's Office Curriculum Review Guide (Graduate Programs)

*Derived from 2 years of Registrar's Office reviewer comments on graduate program
proposals in CourseLeaf CIM (2024-07 → 2026-07). Source: 2,779 workflow comments
across 516 programs; the rules below are the recurring, policy-bearing patterns.
Registrar's Office authors: Heather Daly (primary), David Rogers (Banner setup),
Mariana De Paula, MJ Boudreault, Benoy Joseph, Christine Cornwall, Melanie Standig,
Melissa Couch-Hrinda, Melissa Daigle-Briggs.*

**How to use this.** These are conventions the Registrar's Office applies during
review that are not fully published elsewhere. Aligning a proposal to them *before*
submission avoids rollbacks and speeds approval. Each rule states the convention,
why it exists, and a real example from a reviewed program.

---

## 1. Effective term & catalog edition

**1.1 — The effective catalog edition must match the effective term.** A Fall YYYY
effective term maps to the YYYY–(YYYY+1) catalog edition.
> "Effective catalog edition corrected from 2025-2026 to 2026-2027, per proposed effective term (Fall 2026)." — *Civil Engineering w/ Concentration, 2025-04-17*

**1.2 — Program-requirement changes take effect on a catalog-year (Fall) basis only.**
A requirement change cannot be effective mid-year (e.g. Spring); the effective term is moved to the next Fall.
> "Eff term updated from Spring 2026 to Fall 2026, as program requirement changes can only be made on a catalog year basis." — *Bioinformatics, MS (Boston), 2025-05-05*

**1.3 — There is an annual college-approval deadline per catalog year; miss it and the proposal rolls to the next edition.** The Registrar moves the edition forward when the deadline has passed.
> "As the deadline for college approval of program proposals for 2026-2027 implementation has passed, the Registrar's Office has changed the effective catalog edition of this proposal to 2027-2028." — *Bioinformatics, Graduate Certificate, 2026-04-14 (B. Joseph)*

**1.4 — New degree programs needing Faculty Senate / Board approval may be pushed to a later term/edition**, but can be included in a catalog *addendum* if fully approved before publication.
> "Effective term moved from Fall 2026 to Spring 2027 … given the need for Senate and Board approval … If fully approved before publication of the addendum … the program will be included in that addendum." — *Applied Education Research, MEd (Online), 2026-05-29*

## 2. Program title & naming

**2.1 — Campus-specific program records must carry the campus in the full program title, in parentheses.** Per the Registrar's "campus-specific records project."
> "'(Boston)' added to full program title for campus-specific program record projects." — *Agile Project Management, Graduate Certificate, 2024-08-29*

**2.2 — Degree and campus are both reflected in the full program title.**
> "Degree and campus added to full program title." — *Human-Computer Interaction, MS (Boston), 2026-02-02*

**2.3 — Minors do NOT carry a campus parenthetical.**
> "'(New York)' removed [from] full program title, as Northeastern minors will not have a [campus]…" — *Dance, Minor, 2026-04-14*

**2.4 — Online program titles follow the standard "(Online)" convention.** The special "(Online – Vancouver Requirements)" form is a one-off regulatory case and should **not** be used as a model for new online programs.
> "…the current MS-HEIN-O record … should not be a model for future online programs." — *Health Informatics, MS (Charlotte), 2025-11-14*

---

## 3. Program overview language

**3.1 — Remove implied promises of student achievement / outcomes.**
> "Overview has been modified … to remove implied promises of student achievement." — *Artificial Intelligence, MS—Align, 2026-04-24*

**3.2 — Do not imply the curriculum includes remedial or undergraduate coursework.**
> "The following sentence has been removed … to avoid inadvertently implying that the curriculum includes remedial or undergraduate coursework: 'The Align courses prepare students for the MS AI degree.'" — *Artificial Intelligence, MS—Align, 2026-05-09*

**3.3 — Overview must state whether a concentration is optional or required, consistently with the requirements.** If optional, say "optional"; if a single mandatory concentration, integrate it into the text.
> "We have added the word 'optional' in the subheading of the overview regarding concentrations, to underscore that a concentration is not required." — *Business Administration, MBA—Part-Time, 2026-04-09*

**3.4 — Overview links for multi-campus programs must point to the network-wide listing, not a single campus.**
> "…the link in the overview to the EdD program (which had pointed solely to the Boston program) has been revised to a link to the college's professional doctorate programs, where all network versions … appear." — *Applied Education Research, MEd (Online), 2026-01-15*

---

## 4. Concentrations

**4.1 — If a concentration is not required to complete the program, the heading is not "Concentrations."** It becomes "Concentration or Electives Option," and instructions like "Complete one of the following concentrations" are removed.
> "'Concentrations' heading reverted to 'Concentration or Electives Option', as a concentration is not required for completion of this program." — *Bioinformatics, MS (Oakland), 2025-05-12*

**4.2 — The internal-use "Concentration(s)" field must be populated**, including **NOCC (No Concentration)** when none is required, and the "Can students choose this concentration at the time of admission?" flag set consistently across the program's concentrations.
> "NOCC (No Concentration) added to 'Concentration(s)' field, as a concentration is not required to earn the degree nor required to be declared at time of admission." — *Business Administration, MBA (Online), 2025-05-23*

**4.3 — Inactive courses inside a concentration must be resolved before approval** (proposal is routed back to the college dean's office to fix).
> "Proposal moved to CPS dean's office for resolution of inactive courses in Applied Machine Intelligence Concentration." — *Analytics, MPS (Portland), 2025-10-23*

**4.4 — Concentration requirements should be consistent across a program's campus deployments** (align non-Boston records to the Boston record).
> "Update to the Insurance Analytics concentration requirements, per the same concentration's requirements on the Boston record for this program." — *Analytics, MPS (Toronto), 2025-11-04*

## 5. Credit hours

**5.1 — The catalog publishes only the MINIMUM hours required to complete the program** — ranges like "34–35 SH" become "34 SH". Exception: Align/Bridge/Connect programs, which may legitimately require more.
> "The original 34-35 SH range … changed to just 34 SH, per current catalog practice to document only the min required hours (except for Align/Bridge/Connect programs)." — *Analytics, MPS (Seattle), 2025-09-09*

**5.2 — Restricted-elective / choice language uses "at least N SH" / "a minimum of N SH," not a range.**
> "'Complete 4-5 SH from the following…' → 'Complete at least 4 SH from the following…'" — *Analytics, MPS (Seattle), 2026-01-06*

**5.3 — Remove "maximum of N semester hours" statements.** The catalog documents the minimum for completion; students may exceed it where options allow.
> "…we have removed '…may complete a maximum of 15 semester hours', as the catalog publishes the minimum required for certificate completion." — *Accounting and Financial Decision Making, Graduate Certificate, 2026-04-17*

**5.4 — Credit hours must be semester hours (SH), not quarter hours (QH).** QH references are corrected (part of the CPS semester conversion).
> "Quarter hours reference in Program Credit / GPA Requirements section corrected to semester hours." — *Applied Machine Intelligence, Graduate Certificate, 2025-10-21*

**5.5 — For "choose N SH from the following" requirements, put the hours in the dedicated hours field and indent the options** so credit is associated with the requirement, not shown per-option. Conversely, **core-requirement courses are un-indented** so their hours display.
> "3 semester hours added in dedicated hours field … Course options then indented thereafter, so that 3 SH is associated with the requirement and not with each option." — *Analytics, MPS (Boston), 2025-10-14*

## 6. Committee routing (UGCC / UUCC / Graduate Council)

**6.1 — Any change to core requirements must be routed to UGCC (graduate).**
> "To UGCC for changes to core requirements." — *Accounting and Business Administration, 2026-05-27*

**6.2 — Adding courses from other colleges (or adding/removing a college's course from a UIP) requires UGCC review.**
> "To UGCC for addition of course options from Khoury and Bouve." — *Bioinformatics, Graduate Certificate, 2026-05-08*

**6.3 — A "Course Not Found" error in a curriculum clears once the referenced new course is approved by UGCC** — it is not a defect to fix in the program proposal.
> "…currently producing a 'Course Not Found' error … has now been fully approved by UGCC and the course details will populate…" — *Artificial Intelligence, MS (Miami), 2025-04-09*

**6.4 — Online programs do not require GRA review** and are routed to the Registrar's degree-audit team instead.
> "Workflow correction. Proposal moved from GRA to Registrar's Office degree audit team. No GRA review required for Online programs." — *Health Informatics, MS (Online), 2026-02-04*

---

## 7. Admission, inactivation & teach-out

**7.1 — Set "Is this program open for students applying through admissions?" correctly** — but note PlusOne students are admitted via Slate, so a program admitting PlusOne students must remain "Yes" even if not open to external applicants.
> "I have changed the field … from 'No' to 'Yes', as … PlusOne students are indeed presently admitted to graduate programs via Slate…" — *Computer Science, MSCS (Oakland), 2026-06-23*

**7.2 — An inactivation proposal is held until its successor new-program proposal is ready; both advance to the same UGCC meeting.**
> "This program inactivation proposal will be held until the college is ready to advance the successor new MS program proposal. … advanced to the same UGCC meeting." — *Biomedical Science, MS (Boston), 2025-12-11*

**7.3 — The "Final Catalog" field on an inactivation must match the inactivation's effective term.** Inactivated as of Fall 2025 → final catalog 2024-2025 (not 2025-2026).
> "As the program was authorized for inactivation as of Fall 2025, the final catalog was 2024-2025 rather than 2025-2026." — *Cloud Software Development, Graduate Certificate, 2025-06-30*

## 8. Degree audit, CIP codes & campus/modality

**8.1 — CIP code and campus/modality fields are set by the Registrar's degree-audit team** on new program records (an administrative step, not a college action).
> "Registrar's Office administrative action to add CIP Code and campus to new program record fields." — *Analytics, MPS (Portland), 2025-08-19*

**8.2 — An online program flipping BOS → Online (VTL) for 2026-2027 is not a genuinely new program**, even though CourseLeaf tags it "New Program Proposal" — it's the creation of the VTL-campus record. Registrar adds the Banner program/major code and existing CIP.
> "Although CourseLeaf tags this as a 'New Program Proposal', this program is active and is not new. … will now carry campus Online (VTL) as of the 2026-2027 catalog." — *Engineering Management, Graduate Certificate, 2025-10-17*

**8.3 — Dual-degree programs have no degree-audit impact** and skip that step.
> "No degree audit impacts (dual degree program). Advance to Editor and Catalog Setup." — *Business Administration, MBA—Part-Time, 2026-06-11*

**8.4 — Credit-bearing experiential-learning courses that also satisfy an elective must be listed in the electives list** for degree audits to function.
> "The Registrar's Office has added the credit-bearing courses that fulfill the experiential learning requirement to the electives list … necessary for proper functioning of future degree audits." — *Cell and Gene Therapies, MS (Boston), 2026-05-12*

## 9. Grade / GPA requirements

**9.1 — Minimum-grade statements at core requirements use the college's standard wording.** For COE: "The College of Engineering requires that students must earn a grade of C or higher in all required core courses…" — added/normalized per college request.
> "Min grade in core requirements statement further modified … per COE approval on other 26-27 records…" — *Information Systems, MSIS, 2026-06-05*

**9.2 — Prerequisites outside the program should not be required for core courses.** The minimum-grade statement is simplified to "A grade of C or higher is required for each of the following courses."
> "…prerequisites outside of the program should not be required for core courses…" — *Data Architecture and Management, 2026-05-22*

**9.3 — Don't add "prerequisite conditions may require a higher minimum grade" when the program has only one core course** (the sentence is dropped as inapplicable).
> "Second sentence re min grade … removed, as this program has only one core requirement…" — *Information Systems, MSIS, 2026-06-05*

---

## 10. Prerequisites

**10.1 — When elective options require prerequisites outside the program, add the standard alert language** and ensure there are completion pathways that don't require the external coursework.
> "'Some elective options require completion of prerequisites outside of this program.'" — *Data Analytics Engineering, 2026-05-05*

---

## 11. Experiential learning / co-op

**11.1 — If co-op/experiential participation adds hours or tuition, the Program Requirements must alert students.**
> "A statement has been added to the Program Requirement area to alert students that additional coursework may be needed for participation in co-op depending on concentration." — *Artificial Intelligence, MS, 2026-03-26*

**11.2 — Optional experiential learning is placed in a dedicated "Optional Experiential Learning Opportunities" area** (added with college authorization).
> "'Optional Experiential Learning Opportunities' curriculum area added to program requirements per authorization of C. Bolick." — *Applied AI, MPS (Toronto), 2026-03-17*

**11.3 — Non-credit / non-existent co-op prep courses are removed from the curriculum.** A line is deleted if the course was never created or awards no credit.
> "The Registrar's Office removed the line about a non-credit coop course as the course was not created." — *Artificial Intelligence, MS, 2026-04-22 (D. Rogers)*

---

## 12. Thesis / capstone

**12.1 — Zero-credit special-purpose courses (Thesis Continuation, Research) are removed from elective lists** — they don't award credit toward the elective hours.
> "INFO 7986 Research and INFO 7996 Thesis Continuation removed from options to fulfill elective requirements … these 0 credit courses will not advance the student toward … the required elective hours." — *Information Systems, MSIS, 2026-04-17*

**12.2 — A heading offering "Thesis" must reflect what is actually offered** — e.g. "Capstone or Thesis" → "Capstone or Project" when thesis is no longer an option.
> "Changed the heading 'Capstone or Thesis' to 'Capstone or Project' as the[sis] is no longer an option." — *Digital Media, MPS—Connect, 2026-06-04 (C. Cornwall)*

**12.3 — A thesis sequence that relies on a repeatable course requires that course to be UGCC-approved as repeatable first.**
> "The Thesis Sequence cannot be completed as proposed … until the department proposes (and receives UGCC approval) to change the ARTG 7910 course to a repeatable course." — *Design, MFA (Boston), 2025-08-28*

---

## 13. Dual / joint degrees

**13.1 — Dual-degree programs have no standalone degree-audit impact** (see 8.3) but **changes to shared credits still go to UGCC.**
> "To UGCC for change in shared credits in this dual degree pathway." — *Pharmacy, PharmD—Direct Entry, 2025-09-09*

**13.2 — Dual-degree catalog URLs get a redirect** established by the Registrar when program pages move.
> "Redirect established from …/dual-degrees/law-llm-business-administration-mba/… to …/dual-degrees/" — *Law, LLM / Business Administration, MBA, 2025-12-17 (M. De Paula)*

---

## 14. Curriculum-list hygiene

**14.1 — No duplicate courses in a list; electives are kept in alphanumeric order.**
> "Removed one instance of CS 7680 as the course was listed twice in the same course list." — *Computer Science, MSCS—Align, 2026-06-11 (C. Cornwall)*

**14.2 — Inactive ("red-boxed") courses must be resolved by the college before approval;** the Registrar removes inactive courses from requirement options.
> "Attention College of Science, Khoury … and Bouve …, please address the red-boxed inactive courses in your concentrations." — *Complex Network Analysis, 2026-02-27 (D. Rogers)*

**14.3 — Lines for courses that don't exist are removed** (phantom courses that were never created).
> "The Registrar's Office removed the line about a non-credit coop prep course as it does not exist." — *Artificial Intelligence, MS, 2026-04-22 (D. Rogers)*

## 15. Banner setup & program/concentration coding *(Registrar-performed — informational)*

These are administrative actions the Registrar's Office performs; colleges don't do them, but understanding them explains what the Registrar needs and why records look the way they do.

**15.1 — The Registrar assigns the CIP code** (choosing a DHS STEM-list code where the program qualifies) and the campus/modality fields on new records.
> "I gave the 30.3301 Sustainability Studies CIP code which also appears on the DHS STEM list." — *Applied Sustainability, MS (Boston), 2026-06-15 (D. Rogers)*

**15.2 — The Registrar creates the Banner program record**, including a separate **Virtual (VTL)** record for online modalities; when a program exists in two virtual modalities the codes are distinguished with a **"PO"** suffix.
> "Created Virtual program in Banner." — *Applied Logistics, MPS (Online), 2026-01-26 (D. Rogers)*
> "…this program is getting the 'PO' at the end of the code to distinguish the two virtual modalities." — *Cell and Gene Therapies, MS, 2026-04-27 (D. Rogers)*

**15.3 — Concentrations are added/removed in Banner to match the approved curriculum**, and a concentration is **unchecked in Banner to prevent admission** into it.
> "RABS … was in Banner already with Machine Learning, so I unchecked it so that no one could be admitted." — *Artificial Intelligence, MS, 2026-05-22 (D. Rogers)*

**15.4 — For online-only programs, the Boston Banner instance is ended** as of the conversion term.
> "Ended Boston instance in Banner for 202710. This program is Online Only." — *Cannabis Science, Graduate Certificate, 2026-01-05 (D. Rogers)*
