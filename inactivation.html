Graduate Program Portfolio

Using this page

Focus (left rail)

  • Every card, the chart, and the list follow these four settings.
  • Credential — Master’s (the default) or Certificates; clear it for all graduate programs.
  • Grain — Whole programs (the default) counts each program once across all its campuses; Deployments counts each campus separately.
  • College — the program’s owning college. Campus — for whole programs, any program with a deployment at that campus; for deployments, that campus only.

The cards

  • Approved — every program whose inactivation has cleared governance: approved but not yet in effect, in teach-out now, or fully inactivated since the first charted year. Click it to list all three, each labelled with its status and its stop-admitting term.
  • Stopped admitting since 22–23 — programs whose CIM effective term (the first term with no new students) falls in the charted years. The list labels each one Fully inactivated or Teach-out, with the term it stopped.
  • In teach-out now — effective term reached (no new students), inactivation not yet complete. The list shows each program’s current CIM step.
  • Fully inactivated since 22–23 — inactivation completed in CIM, effective in a charted year. The list shows the catalog year it was inactivated and the term it stopped admitting.
  • New programs since 22–23 — programs first listed in the catalog after 22–23. The list shows the first year each was offered.
  • Still admitting — programs taking new students in the latest catalog year, with the change since the start of 22–23. (This card doesn’t open a list.)
  • The cards are stages of one process, so a program can be counted in more than one. The highlighted card is the one the list is showing.

The chart

  • On the books (green line) — programs offered that catalog year, including those in teach-out. Still admitting (dashed amber line) — the same, minus programs past their stop-admitting term. Left axis.
  • Bars, right axis, per catalog year: New (first year offered; none for the first year), Approved (the year its inactivation was approved in CIM), Stopped admitting (the year of its effective term), and Fully inactivated (its last catalog year — it drops off the on-books line the following year).
  • Pipeline now (last column) — programs on their way out today, stacked by stage: Recommended/considering (a leadership, OTP, or college recommendation, no CIM proposal yet), College agreed, Governance (CIM inactivation proposal in review), Approved (approved, effective term still ahead, still admitting), and Teach-out.
  • Hover a bar or point to see its count.

The list

  • Shows what is selected: a card, a catalog year, or Pipeline now. It opens on the latest year. Select a year by clicking it on the chart or in the buttons under the chart; the selected year is shaded.
  • A year — that year’s fully inactivated, approved, stopped-admitting, and new programs, each labelled. The line under the title gives the change in still admitting and on the books from the year before.
  • Pipeline now — each program’s stage, its current CIM step or disposition, its stop-admitting term once approved, and (once a second monthly snapshot exists) whether it advanced since last month.
  • Columns: Program, owning College, Campus(es), and the status for the selection. Click a heading to sort ascending; double-click to sort descending.
  • ⤓ Export list (CSV) — downloads the list as shown, in its current order.

ⓘ About the data explains where each number comes from and what is left out.

About the data

How many graduate programs Northeastern offers each catalog year, which programs were new or fully inactivated, and which are on their way out now. Graduate programs only.

The chart

  • On the books — programs offered that catalog year, including ones in teach-out. Still admitting leaves out programs from the year they enter teach-out.
  • Approved — the year an inactivation was approved in CIM: for a completed inactivation, its final approved CIM version (the workflow’s end, which can trail the governance vote slightly); for one still in progress, when it moved past governance.
  • New / Stopped admitting / Fully inactivated — a program stops admitting at its CIM effective term (no assumed teach-out length) and is fully inactivated in its effective catalog year, matching the cards above. It is still listed in the catalog that year, so it drops off the on-books line the following year. Still admitting changes by new minus stopped admitting. The first charted year has no New bar because there’s no earlier comparable catalog.
  • Pipeline now — programs on their way out today, by stage: Recommended/considering → College agreed → Governance → Approved (inactivation approved, effective term still ahead, still admitting) → Teach-out (effective term reached, no new students).

The numbers at the top

  • Approved — every program whose inactivation has cleared governance: not yet in effect (effective term still ahead), in teach-out, or fully inactivated since the first charted year. It includes everything in the cards that follow.
  • Stopped admitting — programs whose CIM effective term (the first term with no new students) falls since the start of the first charted year.
  • Fully inactivated — inactivation completed in CIM, effective since the first charted year. In teach-out now — effective term reached (no new students), inactivation not yet complete. Programs approved but not yet in effect are in the pipeline as Approved.
  • These are stages of the same process, so a program can appear in more than one. They count CIM inactivation records, including versions retired while the program continues (e.g. Experiential).
  • New programs — the added bars, summed. Still admitting — the latest year, with its change since the start of the first charted year.

Where it comes from

  • Graduate Catalog — each year’s “Major CIP Codes” list of programs accepting students (archived PDFs, plus the live site for the current edition). Entries sharing a Banner code are one program, so renames and modality variants aren’t double-counted.
  • CIM (CourseLeaf) — inactivation completions, teach-out, and workflow stage. A whole program retires only when every campus has.
  • OTP / leadership / college disposition (editable in the tracker) — drives the Recommended/considering and College agreed stages before a CIM proposal exists.
  • Campus deployments — each campus’s own CIM record. CIM doesn’t record when a campus launched, so a campus counts as new only when its whole program is new.

Not included

Undergraduate programs; exit master’s programs (awarded on the way out of a PhD, no direct admission); optimization-track programs; concentration-split records. Mills College programs aren’t counted as additions (they predate their NU catalog listing).
